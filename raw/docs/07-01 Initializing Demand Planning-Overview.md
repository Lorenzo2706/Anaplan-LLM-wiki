# Initializing Demand Planning-Overview

Exercise seven.

Initializing demand planning.

In this exercise, we will begin to set up the demand planning model in order to have the demand planning application working.

This would involve some of the similar model setup tasks that we've experienced with demand analysis, data hub and statistical forecasting such as time settings, hierarchy levels, and source model connections.

Once we've got the basics in place, we will then bring into demand planning the data that we need from the data hub hierarchies and order history.

Along with the data which demand planning sources from two of the other models, on demand analysis and statistical forecasting.

Once we get to the end of this exercise, we will be in a position where the demand planning application is up and running and ready to use.

We should be able to confirm that by looking at some of the initial pages.

So the task that we will step through will be to, first of all, set up the source model connections so that demand planning is pointing at the right demand analysis, step forecasting and data hub.

We will then get the time settings correctly defined along the lines of what we have in the other applications, and we will also then begin to look at the hierarchies and bringing those over to demand planning.

As part of doing that, we will talk about and we will look at the concept of hierarchy reconciliation, the ability to ensure that hierarchies are kept in sync between models, and therefore not needing to run big processes to delete out their contents.

Once the hierarchies have been imported into demand planning, we can then bring over the associated data from the data hub.

On demand analysis and from statistical forecasting.
