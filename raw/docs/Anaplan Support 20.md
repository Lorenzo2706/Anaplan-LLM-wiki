Don't change the module name in Anaplan as this may break the connection.

[Best Practices About Excel Add-In](https://community.anaplan.com/t5/Best-Practices/Best-Practices-About-Excel-Add-In/ta-p/65668)

It's best to use named ranges rather than relying on formula references.

[Best Practices About Microsoft Office for the Add-Ins](https://community.anaplan.com/t5/Best-Practices/Best-Practices-About-Microsoft-Office-for-the-Add-Ins/ta-p/65670)

It is best to upgrade to the latest version after it's been fully tested by the business.
