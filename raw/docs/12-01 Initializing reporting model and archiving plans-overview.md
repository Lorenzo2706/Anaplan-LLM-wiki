# Initializing reporting model and archiving plans-overview

Exercise 12.

Initializing the reporting model and archiving demand plans.

This is the last exercise relating to demand planning, and we will complete the demand planning process by looking at what it takes to configure the archiving of the plan, in order to then subsequently measure things such as forecast accuracy.

As part of this, we will need to initialize the reporting model, which is where the demand plan gets archived to.

So we will look at the tasks associated with that set up of the model.

And then we will explore how we can archive the plans and whether we should automate that or leave it as a manual exercise.

The steps that we will go through is to, first of all, set up the source models for the reporting model so that it is pointed at the right source.

Demand planning model.

We will configure its time settings and its time ranges, and then seed it with the product and customer hierarchies and demand history.

Once we have completed those tasks, we will then be able to start archiving plans into it, which will first of all ensure that it is synchronized with the demand model to recognize its scenarios and current period.

Then we can create the archive and confirm that an archive is available.
