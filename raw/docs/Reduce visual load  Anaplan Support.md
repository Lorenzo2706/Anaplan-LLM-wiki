English

Fewer items on a page enable faster decision-making.

[Top 5 Design Best Practices for UX—Desktop and Mobile](https://community.anaplan.com/t5/Best-Practices/Top-5-Design-Best-Practices-for-UX-Desktop-and-Mobile/ta-p/81648)

When working on your landing pages, try to minimize or summarize the data on these pages and hyperlink to more detailed pages.

- [3.01-03 Summaries first](https://support.anaplan.com/summaries-first-b88e4c5c-6e6f-4705-9f5e-42f1e2e1fead)

Set a default color palette that's approved by your company and works for your users. Also, be consistent throughout the entire Anaplan application in order for the pages to flow and not be disjointed.

Consider using multiple pages instead of putting all elements on one and forcing the user to scroll.
