## Key takeaways

- Two configuration paths: **NUX Card config panel** (new UX) vs. **module toolbar / classic dashboard**.
- Four styles in NUX: Background, Border, Font, Morse.
- The **formatted LI** and **value-source LI** can differ — enabling form validation patterns.
- Custom views strip source-module CF; saved views inherit it.
- Module-level CF is saved on a **view** (must save a view to persist).
- Classic dashboards deprecated for new customers.
