# Trigonometry and Maths Functions

Standard trig + the two math constants.

## Members
- **Trig:** `SIN`, `COS`, `TAN`, `ASIN`, `ACOS`, `ATAN`
- **Hyperbolic:** `SINH`, `COSH`, `TANH`, `ASINH`, `ACOSH`, `ATANH`
- **Conversion:** `TODEGREES`, `TORADIANS`
- **Constants:** `E()`, `PI()`

## Notes
- All trig functions take and return radians. Use `TORADIANS()` to convert from degrees first.
- Rarely used in financial planning; common in operations and engineering models (e.g., calculating distances, scheduling cyclical demand).
