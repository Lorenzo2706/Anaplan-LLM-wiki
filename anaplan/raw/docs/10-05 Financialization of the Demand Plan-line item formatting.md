# Financialization of the Demand Plan-line item formatting

One last consideration to point out is you will see that all of the financials that we are showing are prefixed with a dollar symbol.

In order to adjust that, that is the line item formatting that we see there.

And actually, really the only way to amend that is to change the line item formats themselves.

Quickest way to do that is typically, to go into the full line item inventory within the model’s blueprint.

Select and copy all of those formats into Excel and do a bulk find and replace to change the format from dollars to the currency of choice, and then paste that back.
