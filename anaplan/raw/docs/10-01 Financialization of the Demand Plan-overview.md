# Financialization of the Demand Plan-overview

Exercise ten.

Financialization of the demand plan.

In this exercise we will look at how we convert the demand plan from volumes into value.

Everything we've done so far has very much been based upon the unit demand.

And now we will convert that to both revenue and cost of sales, and hence a gross profit.

As part of doing this, we will look at how we need to import the associated data and what the parameters are, to allow us to apply those pricing and costing information to our volumes.

We will also look at how we can go about adjusting those values as part of the demand planning process, should that be appropriate.

By the end of the exercise, we will get to a place where our demand is now also represented in monetary terms.

So the steps we will go through is to first of all import a pricing file.

So a sales price file into the data hub.

We will then do the same for a unit cost data set.

Importing that into the data hub.

We can review it within the data hub and then transfer it across to the demand planning application.

Once that pricing and costing information appears in demand planning, we should be able to confirm that our volumes are converted into values.

And then finally, we will look at the ways in which we can override and uplift those volumes of value within demand planning.
