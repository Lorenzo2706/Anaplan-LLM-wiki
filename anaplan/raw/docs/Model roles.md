Model roles enable you to tailor model content to the needs of specific business functions, enhance security, and streamline processes. When you control permissions through roles, rather than individual users, you can modify access quickly and flexibly.

This feature [controls access within a model](https://help.anaplan.com/8c495688-216e-49c4-aa56-a4b70487e5d7). You can assign [administration roles](https://help.anaplan.com/84718424-a9ea-4d55-a25b-a1ee2e73e7f4) to control access within your tenant from **Administration**.

Every model contains the **Full Access** and **No Access** roles by default.

When you add a user to a workspace from within a model, you can select their model role for that model. If you add a workspace administrator, they have **Full Access** to all other models in the workspace by default. Other users have **No Access** to other models by default.

If you [provision access to a model](https://help.anaplan.com/1ec79e9c-2927-4f30-9c2f-9c062352933c) from **Administration**, all users have **No Access** by default.

After you add the user, you can change their model role in each individual model to suit your needs.

Workspace administrators can create as many roles as their use case requires and assign different permissions and data access to each.

Once you [add a model role](https://help.anaplan.com/d5499f58-bc52-4b00-aa1d-5c4db46e8b46), you can assign [module](https://help.anaplan.com/2a38c1c2-6338-4ac4-9a45-673d23f2a81a), [version](https://help.anaplan.com/ce68352c-cd18-4190-910a-a4f041c0f3c4), [list](https://help.anaplan.com/2a2c1ccd-b2d4-420c-b58d-3a24f0e03295), and [action](https://help.anaplan.com/9d13d85d-5d7e-4d01-899c-52784cabd9b9) permissions to the role.

You can also [select the landing dashboard](https://help.anaplan.com/ebf9497c-9760-4762-9910-c25712cc2479) for a model role and the content you want to display in the [model **Contents**](https://help.anaplan.com/d8c2dccb-5c98-4638-a4d3-53a9fb94e059) panel.

After you add your model roles, assign permissions, and select the contents and landing dashboard, you can [assign your users to roles](https://help.anaplan.com/8dc4f84f-7760-4654-b2a2-eae7375e662e).
